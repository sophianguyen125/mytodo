Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Mytodo is a simple to-do app. It has no server of its own. Your tasks belong to you, and nobody else can see them.

Where your tasks are stored

  • In your browser on your device.
  • In your own Google Drive, if you sign in with Google. Mytodo uses a hidden app folder that only Mytodo can open. It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

What Mytodo uses from your Google account

  • Your name, email and profile picture: only to show which account you are signed in with.
  • Mytodo's hidden folder in Google Drive: to save your tasks and keep your devices in sync.
  • Google Tasks, only if you turn on "Link to Google Calendar": to show tasks that have a day in your Google Calendar.

What Mytodo does not do

  • It does not sell, share or send your data to anyone.
  • It has no ads and no tracking or analytics.

The AI button

When you use the ✨ AI button, the text you wrote is copied and opened on claude.ai, but only after you click "Copy & open Claude". Nothing is sent to claude.ai without your click.

Removing your data

Sign out and choose "Remove from this device" to delete your tasks from the browser. To remove Mytodo's data from Google Drive, open Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Mytodo → Options → Delete hidden app data. If you used "Link to Google Calendar", you can also delete the "Mytodo" list in Google Tasks. To stop Mytodo's access completely, open your Google Account → Security → Third-party apps, and remove Mytodo.
